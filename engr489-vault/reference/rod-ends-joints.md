# Rod ends & tube joint

Current state. Overwrite as things change; log changes in [[worklog]].

- **Rod ends:** Female Rod End **SI6T/K, M6×1.0** spherical plain bearings.
  ⚠️ **Steel ball — accepted compromise for the first prototype ONLY, non-magnetic swap
  planned.** This is the one deliberate exception to the non-magnetic rule; don't let it
  carry silently into the final build. → [[open-issues]]
- **Carbon fibre tube:** 6 mm OD / 4 mm ID.
- **Thread/tube mismatch:** M6 female thread minor Ø 4.95 mm vs 6 mm CF tube OD.
  **Resolution:** bore out the M6 thread in the rod-end shank and epoxy the CF tube
  directly into the bore (direct-bonded, no stud or ferrule).
- **Constraint:** right-hand thread on both ends → no turnbuckle adjustment.
- **Still needed:** a bonding jig for the parallelogram rod pairs. → [[open-issues]]
